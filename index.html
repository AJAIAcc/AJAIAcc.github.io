Minerva
A private personal assistant

What Minerva is

Minerva is a personal assistant built and run by Ajaydas Devadas for his own use. It is not a public service: there are no other users, no sign-ups and nothing for sale.

It runs on a private server and talks to its owner through Telegram and a companion Android app. It uses an AI model to help him keep track of his day: email, documents, calendar, reminders, travel and news.

How it uses Google

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's own sign-in, Minerva can:

  • Gmail: read and search his email, summarise what needs attention, organise it (labels, read/unread, archive), and draft replies, which are sent only after he approves each one.
  • Google Docs: find and read his documents, create new ones, and propose edits to existing ones, which are applied only after he approves them.
  • Google Drive: search and read his files (read-only; Minerva cannot change, delete or share them).

Google data is used only to provide these features to the owner. See the privacy policy for details.
