An AI front desk for your business

What it does

  • Answers questions about your services, prices, hours and policies, in your customer's language. It only says what you've told it: if it doesn't know, it asks you instead of guessing.
  • Takes bookings. Requests arrive in your Telegram with Confirm and Decline buttons, and the customer is told either way. It never confirms anything by itself.
  • Hands over to you when a customer asks for a person, complains, wants a refund, or needs anything delicate. You reply straight from your phone.
  • Keeps a record of every booking request and enquiry, which you can download as a spreadsheet.
  • Stays within budget, with a daily spending limit and protection against spam.

How it works

  1. You tell me about your business: a short questionnaire covering services, prices, hours, booking rules and what should always come to you.
  2. I set it up, and we test it together on your phone until the answers are right. Usually within a week.
  3. It runs. You tap Confirm on bookings and step in whenever you like. When your prices or hours change, send me the new details.
